Manage Your Subscription

You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your MainLine Pro subscription at any time through your device account settings.

  • iOS: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → MainLine
  • Android: Google Play → Account → Subscriptions → MainLine

Refund Requests

Refunds are handled directly by Apple or Google. To request a refund, visit the relevant platform's support page.

Delete Your Data

All of your data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time from within the app:

Settings → Data → Delete All Data. This permanently removes your repertoire, drill history, and settings. Alternatively, uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does MainLine decide what to show me?
After each drill, MainLine schedules the next review based on how well you played. Strong lines wait longer; shaky lines come back sooner.

How does the streak work?
You earn a streak day whenever you complete at least one drill session. Missing a day resets the streak to 0.

What is MainLine Pro?
MainLine Pro is an optional subscription that gives you unlimited hearts so you can drill without waiting for refills. See our Terms of Use for full subscription details.

I restored my purchase but Pro isn't showing.
Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase. If the issue persists, contact us.

Can I use MainLine on multiple devices?
Your data is stored locally, so it doesn't sync between devices automatically. You can use the app on any device signed in to your account, but your repertoire and progress are separate on each device.
