Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BunnyHops ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the MainLine mobile application ("MainLine" or the "App").

MainLine is a chess training app that helps users build a repertoire, drill lines, and optionally import weak spots from public Lichess and Chess.com data. MainLine does not require users to create an account with us.

1. Summary

In short:

  • Most MainLine data is stored locally on your device.
  • We do not operate a user account system or a cloud sync backend for MainLine.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs to track user behavior in the App.
  • If you choose to use optional features, limited data may be transmitted to third parties:
    • your Lichess or Chess.com username when you use rating lookup or game analysis features;
    • purchase information through Apple or Google when you buy or restore a subscription;
    • notification permissions and locally scheduled reminders through your device operating system.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on how you use MainLine, the App may handle the following categories of information:

A. Data stored locally on your device

MainLine stores your app data locally on your device, including:

  • your saved repertoire, custom lines, imported lines, and puzzle packs;
  • drill progress, practice history, streak data, hearts state, and review scheduling data;
  • app settings, such as theme preference and notification preference;
  • optional saved chess account usernames for Lichess and/or Chess.com;
  • optional saved rating information and last-analysis timestamps related to those chess accounts;
  • subscription entitlement status used to unlock paid features.

This local data is stored using on-device app storage and is not uploaded to our servers.

B. Data sent when you use public chess account features

If you choose to use MainLine's optional rating lookup or game analysis features, the App sends the username you enter to the relevant public chess service so the App can fetch data you requested.

This may include:

  • your Lichess username;
  • your Chess.com username;
  • publicly available rating information associated with that username;
  • publicly available game archive data associated with that username, when you request game analysis.

We use this information only to provide the feature you requested inside the App, such as recommending puzzle packs or generating training lines based on recent games.

We do not operate the Lichess or Chess.com services, and their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

C. Purchase and subscription information

If you purchase, subscribe to, or restore MainLine Pro, payment processing and subscription management are handled by Apple and/or Google, depending on your platform.

We do not receive your full payment card details. The App may receive limited purchase status information from the platform or in-app purchase provider, such as whether an eligible subscription appears active, so the App can unlock paid features.

D. Notification information

If you enable notifications, MainLine may request notification permission from your device and schedule local reminder notifications. These reminders are used to help you return to practice.

E. Support communications

If you contact us directly, for example by email, we may receive the information you include in your message, such as your name, email address, and the contents of your request.

3. How We Collect Information

We collect or handle information in the following ways:

  • directly from you, when you enter a username, toggle settings, create lines, or contact support;
  • automatically on your device, when the App saves your repertoire, progress, settings, or reminder preferences;
  • from third-party services at your request, when you ask the App to look up a rating or analyze games using Lichess or Chess.com;
  • from Apple or Google, when subscription status is checked or restored through the platform purchase system.

4. How We Use Information

We use information handled by MainLine to:

  • provide the App's core training features;
  • save and restore your repertoire, custom lines, drill history, and app preferences;
  • analyze public chess account data only when you request rating lookup or game analysis;
  • recommend content and generate training material based on your usage choices;
  • enable optional notifications;
  • unlock and maintain paid subscription features;
  • respond to support requests;
  • maintain, debug, and improve the App.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share your data with advertising networks for targeted advertising.

We may share or transmit limited information in the following circumstances:

  • Lichess and Chess.com: If you use optional account lookup or game analysis features, your entered username and related requests are sent to the relevant public API so the feature can work.
  • Apple and Google: If you make or restore an in-app purchase, Apple and/or Google process the transaction and may provide purchase status information to the App.
  • Service providers required for app functionality: The App relies on operating system and platform services, such as local notification frameworks and app store billing systems.
  • Legal reasons: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, regulation, legal process, or valid governmental request, or if necessary to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the App.

6. Data Retention

Because MainLine primarily stores data locally on your device, most app data remains on your device until you delete it.

You can generally remove local app data by:

  • using the in-app "Delete All Data" feature;
  • uninstalling the App;
  • changing or clearing optional chess usernames from your device data.

If you contact support, we may retain support communications for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, keep records of the issue, comply with legal obligations, or enforce our rights.

We do not maintain a MainLine user account database for this App.

7. Your Choices

Depending on how you use the App, you can:

  • choose not to provide a Lichess or Chess.com username;
  • stop using optional account analysis features at any time;
  • disable notifications in the App or in your device settings;
  • manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple or Google account settings;
  • delete local App data through the App's settings or by uninstalling the App;
  • contact us to ask questions about this Privacy Policy.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the App. For example:

  • MainLine primarily stores your training data locally on your device;
  • requests to third-party services are made over network connections intended for app functionality;
  • we do not intentionally collect more data than is needed for the features you use.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

MainLine is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through our own systems.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through support communications or another direct interaction, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to address the issue.

10. International Use

If you use the App outside the country where BunnyHops is based, your information may be processed on your device and transmitted to third-party services such as Apple, Google, Lichess, or Chess.com, which may operate in multiple countries. By using those optional features, you understand that information may be handled in jurisdictions that may have different data protection laws than your own.

11. Third-Party Services

MainLine may link to or rely on third-party services that are not operated by us. We encourage you to review their privacy practices before using those services. Relevant third parties may include:

  • Apple
  • Google
  • Lichess
  • Chess.com

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we will post the updated version at this URL and update the "Effective date" above. Your continued use of MainLine after the updated Privacy Policy becomes effective means you acknowledge the updated policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
