Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the MainLine mobile application and website ("MainLine" or the "App").

MainLine is a chess trainer that helps you practice patterns, build a repertoire, review lines, and optionally analyze public Lichess and Chess.com data. MainLine does not require its own account, and most App data stays on your device.

1. Quick Summary

  • MainLine is designed to work without its own account system.
  • Your repertoire, drills, settings, and progress are primarily stored locally on your device.
  • We use Amplitude for product and website analytics and app performance monitoring.
  • We use Google AdMob to deliver in-app ads.
  • Analytics and ad delivery may involve technical data, including IP address and app/device identifiers.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • MainLine does not currently use IDFA-based cross-app tracking for ad delivery.

2. Information MainLine Handles

A. Data stored locally on your device

MainLine stores most App data locally on your device. Depending on how you use the App, this may include:

  • saved repertoire lines, custom lines, imported lines, and puzzle packs;
  • drill progress, practice history, streak data, hearts state, and review scheduling data;
  • App settings, including theme and notification preferences;
  • optional saved chess account usernames for Lichess and Chess.com;
  • optional cached rating information and last-analysis timestamps related to those accounts; and
  • purchase entitlement status used to unlock paid features.

MainLine does not currently use its own account system or cloud sync service, so this information is not uploaded to our own servers as part of normal App use.

B. Analytics data (Amplitude)

When you use MainLine or visit the website, we may collect analytics data through Amplitude. Depending on your device, browser, and platform configuration, this data may include:

  • IP address;
  • device identifiers and app instance identifiers;
  • app version, operating system, device model, language, and time zone;
  • approximate location inferred from IP address; and
  • in-app events, website page views, outbound link clicks, feature interactions, session timing, and diagnostic context.

C. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

MainLine uses Google AdMob to request and serve ads. When ad requests are made, Google and its ad serving infrastructure may process technical data such as:

  • IP address;
  • app and device identifiers used for ad delivery and fraud prevention;
  • approximate location derived from IP address;
  • ad request, response, and performance metadata; and
  • diagnostic and invalid-traffic detection signals.

MainLine currently requests ads on iOS without using IDFA. Even when ads are not personalized, technical data such as IP address may still be used for basic ad delivery, measurement, and security.

D. Data used for public chess account features

If you choose to use optional rating lookup or game analysis features, the App sends the username you enter to the relevant public chess service so it can fetch the information you requested.

This may include:

  • your Lichess username;
  • your Chess.com username;
  • public rating information associated with that username; and
  • public game archive data associated with that username when you request game analysis.

We use this information only to provide the feature you asked for inside MainLine, such as highlighting weak spots or generating training material from recent games.

Lichess and Chess.com are independent third-party services. Their privacy practices and terms apply to the data they provide and the services they operate.

E. Purchase and subscription information

If you purchase, subscribe to, or restore MainLine Pro, payment processing and subscription management are handled by Apple and/or Google, depending on your platform.

We do not receive your full payment card details. The App may receive limited purchase or entitlement status information from the platform so it can unlock eligible paid features.

F. Notification information

If you enable reminders, MainLine may request notification permission from your device and schedule local notifications to help you return to practice.

G. Support communications

If you contact us directly, such as by email, we may receive the information you include in your message, including your name, email address, device details, and the contents of your request.

3. How Information Is Collected

We collect or handle information in the following ways:

  • directly from you when you enter usernames, create lines, change settings, make a purchase, or contact support;
  • automatically on your device when the App saves your repertoire, progress, settings, or reminder preferences;
  • automatically through analytics instrumentation (including Amplitude) when app usage, website page views, outbound link clicks, and diagnostics events are generated;
  • automatically through ad SDK instrumentation (including Google AdMob) when ad requests and ad measurement events are generated;
  • from third-party services at your request when you ask MainLine to look up ratings or analyze public games; and
  • from Apple or Google when subscription status is checked, restored, or renewed through the platform purchase system.

4. How We Use Information

We use information handled by MainLine to:

  • provide the App's core chess training features;
  • save and restore your local repertoire, drill history, and preferences on your device;
  • analyze app and website usage and performance trends through Amplitude;
  • request, deliver, measure, and protect in-app ad serving through Google AdMob;
  • troubleshoot crashes, errors, and reliability issues;
  • detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or security issues;
  • analyze public chess account data only when you request those features;
  • generate recommendations and training material based on the choices you make in the App;
  • enable optional notifications and reminders;
  • unlock and maintain paid subscription features; and
  • respond to support requests and improve MainLine.

5. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers that help us operate MainLine, including analytics and ad delivery providers.

We may share or transmit limited information only in the following situations:

  • Amplitude: Analytics event and technical metadata are processed by Amplitude on our behalf for product analytics and diagnostics.
  • Google AdMob: Ad request data and related technical metadata may be processed by Google for ad delivery, ad measurement, and invalid-traffic/fraud prevention.
  • Lichess and Chess.com: If you use optional account lookup or game analysis features, your entered username and related requests are sent to the relevant public API so the feature can work.
  • Apple and Google: If you buy, renew, or restore an in-app purchase, Apple and/or Google process the transaction and may provide entitlement status information back to the App.
  • Infrastructure providers: MainLine relies on standard operating system, networking, and app store services to function.
  • Legal or safety reasons: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or valid governmental request, or if disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the App.

6. Retention and Deletion

Because MainLine primarily stores data locally on your device, most App data remains there until you delete it.

You can generally remove local App data by:

  • using the in-app "Delete All Data" feature;
  • clearing or changing optional chess usernames stored in the App; or
  • uninstalling the App from your device.

Analytics data handled through Amplitude is retained according to our configured retention periods and Amplitude's service controls.

Ad-related data handled by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's retention controls and policies.

If you contact support, we may retain support communications for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, keep records of the issue, comply with legal obligations, or enforce our rights.

Purchase records and subscription records are maintained by Apple or Google under their own policies.

7. Your Choices

Depending on how you use MainLine, you can:

  • use the App without providing a Lichess or Chess.com username;
  • stop using optional account analysis features at any time;
  • disable notifications in the App or in your device settings;
  • adjust available ad and privacy settings in your device or Google account, where supported;
  • use any in-app consent flow shown in your region where required by law;
  • manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple or Google account settings;
  • delete local App data through MainLine settings or by uninstalling the App; and
  • contact us if you have privacy questions.

MainLine currently does not provide a separate in-app toggle to opt out of ad delivery. Where required by law, consent options are provided through the applicable consent flow.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by MainLine. For example, the App primarily stores your training data locally on your device and limits external requests to features you choose to use.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. The protection of information stored on your device also depends on your device settings and the security practices of third-party services and vendors you choose to use.

9. Children's Privacy

MainLine is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through our own systems.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support communications or another direct interaction, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to address the issue.

10. International Use

If you use MainLine outside the country where we are based, information may be processed on your device and transmitted to third-party services such as Apple, Google, Google AdMob, Amplitude, Lichess, or Chess.com, which may operate in multiple countries. By using MainLine, you understand that information may be handled in jurisdictions with data protection laws that differ from those in your own country.

11. Third-Party Services

MainLine may link to or rely on third-party services that we do not operate. We encourage you to review their privacy practices before using them. Relevant third parties may include:

  • Amplitude
  • Apple
  • Google
  • Google AdMob
  • Lichess
  • Chess.com

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we will post the updated version at this URL and update the effective date above. Your continued use of MainLine after the updated Privacy Policy becomes effective means you acknowledge the updated policy, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
